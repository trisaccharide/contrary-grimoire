What science is hiding in this spell?

This is additive RGB mixing: light from separate red, green, and blue channels combines on a screen. Red + green makes yellow; green + blue makes cyan; red + blue makes magenta. Balanced channels produce neutral light.

The sliders use screen RGB settings, not measured physical power. Equal low settings look gray; equal maximum settings look white. Glows and beams are decorative. This is a simplified display model, not a calibrated optics simulation.
